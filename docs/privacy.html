Budget Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 July 2026

Budget (“the app”) is a personal budget and credit-card statement tracker. Your financial data stays private on your device. The free version shows ads through Google AdMob; the optional Budget Pro purchase removes them. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

Your financial data stays on your device

Your transactions, budgets, cards, bills, goals, and any statements you import are stored only on your device using Apple’s on-device database. This financial information is never uploaded, sold, or shared — it never leaves your iPhone and is never sent to us or any third party. We have no servers and no user accounts.

Statement import

When you upload a PDF statement, it is read and parsed entirely on your device. The PDF and the transactions extracted from it are not uploaded anywhere.

Advertising (free version)

The free version of Budget shows banner advertisements provided by Google AdMob. To serve these ads, Google’s advertising software may collect limited information such as device identifiers (including the Advertising Identifier), approximate/coarse information, ad interactions, and diagnostic data. This is used to display and measure ads and is handled by Google, not by us. Google’s handling of this data is governed by Google’s policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from AdMob

Your financial data is never shared with Google or used for advertising. Only the standard advertising signals above are involved, and only in the free version.

App Tracking Transparency

On first launch the free version asks, through Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt, whether you allow tracking for more relevant ads. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, the Advertising Identifier is not used to track you and you may see less-relevant ads. Your choice can be changed anytime in iOS Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Tracking.

Budget Pro (in-app purchase)

Budget offers a one-time in-app purchase, Budget Pro, which removes all ads and lifts the free card limit. The purchase is processed by Apple’s App Store; we do not receive or store your payment information. Once you buy Pro, the advertising software described above is not used.

Face ID

If you enable the optional app lock, Budget uses Face ID / Touch ID through iOS only to unlock the app on your device. Biometric data is handled by iOS and is never accessible to the app or to us.

Children

Budget does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
