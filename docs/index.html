Frequently asked

How do I import a statement?
Go to the Cards tab, add your card, open it, then tap Upload Statement (PDF) and pick your bank’s PDF statement. Review the parsed transactions, then save. Everything is processed on your device.

My statement is password-protected and won’t open.
Open the PDF in the Files app, enter the password, then use Share ▸ Print ▸ Save as PDF to make an unlocked copy, and import that.

I deleted a statement — what happens to its transactions?
Transactions the import created are removed with it; transactions you had already entered yourself are kept.

Is my financial data private?
Yes. Your transactions, cards, and statements are stored only on your device and are never uploaded or shared. The free version shows banner ads via Google AdMob (standard advertising data only — never your financial data); see our Privacy Policy.

How do I remove the ads?
Buy Budget Pro — a one-time purchase in Settings ▸ Budget Pro. It removes all ads and lets you add unlimited cards. Already bought it on another device? Tap Restore Purchases.

How many cards can I add?
The free version supports one card. Budget Pro unlocks unlimited cards so you can import statements from all of them.
